Point of view

Commissioning beyond the cycle

Most commissioning problems aren't caused by people forgetting a process. They are produced by the conditions in which the process is being used.

Learning design

Learning changes when the challenge is live

Public service development works differently when participants are accountable for doing something with the learning while the programme is still running.

Tool note

What a Compass is for

A Compass shouldn't tell every organisation where to go. It should help people see where they are, compare bearings and choose a next move.

About PSTA

A social enterprise for public service capability.

The Academy exists to build the capability of public services to collaborate, innovate, commission and transform for better outcomes. It combines serious ideas with practical application and cross-sector peer learning.
