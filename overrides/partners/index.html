Our wider partner history

PSTA was created as a collaborative social enterprise and has developed through relationships across government, local public services, civil society, professional practice and evidence organisations. Organisations that have been partners, founding collaborators or significant contributors include:

Whitehall & Industry Group

A founding collaborator in PSTA's cross-sector approach to leadership and learning.

LocalGov Digital

The network for digital practitioners in local government, bringing practical experience of open, user-centred and digitally enabled public services.

NCVO

Contributor of voluntary and community sector perspectives to public service commissioning and transformation.

Browne Jacobson

Legal and public service expertise, including the practical implications of new models of commissioning and delivery.

E3M

A network focused on innovation, social enterprise and public-benefit models for public service design and delivery.

The Social Innovation Partnership

Evidence, innovation and social-impact expertise used to strengthen how public services learn what works.

Alliance for Useful Evidence / Nesta

Part of PSTA's wider evidence and innovation network, helping connect practical commissioning with better use of evidence.

Other programme relationships

PSTA programmes and resources have also involved organisations including the Local Government Association and specialist systems-thinking and public-service partners. These relationships vary by programme and over time; we describe relevant partners on individual programme pages rather than imply that every past collaboration is a current corporate partnership.
