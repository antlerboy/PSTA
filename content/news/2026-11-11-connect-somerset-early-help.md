**Connect Somerset** is a partnership involving Somerset Council, the NHS, voluntary, community, faith, and social-enterprise organisations, and education settings.

Its public account of the programme describes work on local leadership, sharing information, connecting people with community resources, and organising support earlier. The **2024–2030 blueprint** sets out the intended direction; it should be read as a programme ambition, rather than evidence that every intended benefit has been achieved.

The practical interest for commissioners and service leaders is how support gets organised across existing boundaries. Who can remove a barrier? How do people find local help? What changes when services work around a family's circumstances?

[Read about Connect Somerset and follow its links to the blueprint and impact report](https://connectsomerset.org.uk/about-connect-somerset/).
