John V Willshire’s Community Power Compass, published by Smithery on **15 June 2022**, asks how freedom and control work in communities. It draws on David Graeber and David Wengrow’s *The Dawn of Everything*.

The guide examines freedom to disobey, move, and reorganise, alongside control through influence, information, and the system’s rules. Its questions can help a group discuss whose voice is heard, how people can leave, and who can change the arrangements they share.

For people working with citizens and communities, these are questions to examine in context. Participation in a meeting does not by itself show what people can influence or change. This is the PSTA’s reading of the resource, rather than an evaluation of the tool.

This is an independently developed resource, distinct from the PSTA’s Commissioning Compass and smartCompass.
