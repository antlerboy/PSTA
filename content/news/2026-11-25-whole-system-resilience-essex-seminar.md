How can the relationships between communities and public services strengthen the resilience of the whole system?

At a research seminar organised by Essex Business School on Wednesday 25 November 2026, from 14:00 to 16:00 GMT, Dr Elke Loeffler will explore the evidence on user and community co-production and invite participants to help shape a research agenda.

The seminar is online via Zoom. It is organised by the Centre for Public Accountability and Management.
