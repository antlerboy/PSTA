Think Local Act Personal's **asset-based commissioning hub** is a useful starting point for commissioners exploring how to work with people's strengths and the resources within communities.

The hub brings together an introduction to the key ideas, research and academic writing, stories from practice, and related webinars delivered by Social Care Future. It also invites people to contribute their own experiences and resources.

For commissioning teams, it offers material to read and discuss alongside their own work: how do these ideas relate to the way you understand a place, build relationships, and commission support?

[Explore TLAP's asset-based commissioning hub](https://thinklocalactpersonal.org.uk/our-hubs/asset-based-commissioning/).
