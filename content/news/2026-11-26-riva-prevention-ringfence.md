In a RIVA article published on **4 November 2026**, Fiona Sheil examines the proposed Preventative Departmental Expenditure Limit and its implications for preventing violence against women and girls.

She asks how prevention will be classified, how early work can build evidence, where public costs are recorded, and who has an incentive to fund work whose benefits fall in another department. The article proposes violence against women and girls as a test case for designing the ringfence.

RIVA calls for shared definitions, a participatory system map, and stronger research capacity. For commissioners and system leaders, the questions concern whose experience counts and how investment decisions account for effects beyond the service paying. These are RIVA’s proposals, rather than confirmed delivery arrangements.
