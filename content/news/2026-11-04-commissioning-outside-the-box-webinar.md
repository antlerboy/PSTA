**Thursday 26 November 2026, 13:00–14:00 GMT · Online · Free**

Community Catalysts is hosting an interactive session for commissioners, directors, and managers interested in developing care and support around people's lives.

The session explores micro-commissioning, direct payments, and community micro-enterprises as alternatives to traditional home care and day services. Commissioners from Cheshire West and Chester and Hertfordshire will share their experience of developing local micro-markets with Community Catalysts.

The discussion covers the practical conditions for choice and control: helping people direct their support, giving practitioners confidence to work differently, and enabling small local providers to offer useful alternatives.

**Registration closes on Wednesday 25 November at 17:00 GMT**, according to the booking page checked on 4 November.
