Jennifer Trueland's article in **The Doctor**, published on **30 September 2026**, follows GP Kath Jones and The Flipside, a creative-health centre run by Flip of the Coin in Alness.

The account describes links between general practice and a nearby space for gardening, creative activity, and community life. It includes patients' experiences and the developing research around community assets and health.

For public-service teams, it offers a practical question: how can clinical support and community relationships work together around what matters in someone's life? The reported experiences invite learning; they don't establish a universal result or a guaranteed saving.
