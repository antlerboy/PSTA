Ideas Alliance hosts the five-question *NextGen Commissioning Instincts Quiz*, developed by Jessie Cunnett Consulting Ltd. It invites NHS commissioners to notice which approach they tend to reach for first when balancing governance, performance, partnership, and complex needs. The site says you can see a result without supplying your details.

Try it with colleagues, then compare the choices you made. Where might a different response help? What does each approach make easier to see, and what might it miss? Treat the result as a prompt for discussion, rather than an assessment of someone's capability.

[Use the commissioning instincts quiz on the Ideas Alliance site](https://ideas-alliance.org.uk/commissioning-instincts-quiz/).
