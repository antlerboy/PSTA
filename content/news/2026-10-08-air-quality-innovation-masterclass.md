Connected Places Catapult is running an online Innovation Challenge Masterclass on Wednesday 14 October 2026, from 10:00 to 11:30 UK time.

The session uses Westminster City Council's work with the Innovation Procurement Empowerment Centre to examine how a local authority defined an air-quality problem, engaged the market, tested solutions in a live environment, and generated evidence for future procurement and investment.

The first 45 minutes are a practical masterclass. The second half is a drop-in surgery for local-authority teams working on air quality, implementation barriers, market engagement, and possible trials.

[See the programme and apply to attend](https://cp.catapult.org.uk/event/innovation-challenge-masterclass-tackling-air-quality/).
