What would change if someone knew the neighbourhood, could stay alongside people, and helped connect their own strengths with local relationships and services?

In **'LAC of the future'**, dated **28 July 2026** on the source page, the Local Area Coordination Network sets out a vision of a Local Area Coordinator in every neighbourhood. It describes support that people can approach without a complicated referral process and continue to use without an arbitrary time limit.

The article asks how such a role might help with difficulties that often precede crisis: isolation, caring responsibilities, unemployment, and insecure housing. Its proposal is a vision for discussion, rather than evidence that this coverage already exists or a commitment to a funded national rollout.

It provides useful questions for people designing prevention and neighbourhood support: who can approach, what help is available before thresholds are met, and whether relationships can continue for as long as they are needed.
