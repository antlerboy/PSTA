Q Community's relational-services collection was published in June and updated on 5 October 2026. It brings together introductory resources, reports, webinar and podcast recordings, relevant organisations, and routes into communities of practice.

The collection connects person-centred care with wider questions about learning, continuity, failure demand, public management, and the organisational conditions that relational work needs.

It is useful both as a starting point and as a map of work already under way. The range also makes an important point: relational practice is not one model or branded method, and evidence about particular approaches still needs to be read in context.
