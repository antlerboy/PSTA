Kent County Council's *Winning for Kent: Procurement with Purpose Policy* applies a social-value purpose to the council's substantial purchasing power.

The policy's 2026–28 goals are to support skills and employment, increase opportunities for local businesses and voluntary organisations, and contribute to healthier, safer, and stronger communities.

For commissioners, the important test comes after the policy statement: how purpose is reflected in specifications, market engagement, supplier selection, contract management, and evidence about what changed locally.

[Read the policy summary and download the full policy](https://www.kent.gov.uk/about-the-council/strategies-and-policies/corporate-policies/winning-for-kent-procurement-with-purpose-policy).
