Regular PSTA facilitator and former town mayor Jon Harvey has published a new edition of *The Mayor’s Bear and Other Stories of Hope*.

Alongside the original story, the new edition includes three short stories and teacher notes. The stories explore making playgrounds more welcoming and inclusive, celebrating the achievement of a young refugee, and creating kinder, more dementia-friendly towns. They introduce children — and the adults reading with them — to local government and to the possibility of taking part in shaping their communities.

For public-service practitioners, this is a gentle example of civic learning rooted in imagination, hope and place: helping young people see that they may not be able to change the whole world, but they can help to make one part of it better.
