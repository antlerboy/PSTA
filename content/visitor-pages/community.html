Case clinics on request

Bring a commissioning decision or a change your team has struggled to make. A facilitator helps the group examine the case and the person who brought it chooses what to try. You can request a 90-minute session or a short series.

See the clinic format and host guide or request a session for your group.

Practitioner groups in development

We’re testing interest in two groups, one for commissioning practitioners and one for people leading service transformation. Email info@publicservicetransformation.org if you’d like to hear about either.
