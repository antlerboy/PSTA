About the Academy

The Public Service Transformation Academy is a registered social enterprise. We teach people who commission and run public services.

How the Academy began

The Cabinet Office launched the National Commissioning Academy in 2013. The Public Service Transformation Academy was formed in 2016 and took on the programme. More than 2,500 people have graduated from the Academy and its programmes across government, health, policing, housing, civil society, and other public services.

RedQuadrant is the lead partner. We work with specialist partners on training, simulations, and accredited programmes.

How people learn here

Participants use a decision from work as the subject of their programme. Between sessions they try something with the people involved and bring back what happened. A colleague from another public service can often help explain why a decision that makes sense in one organisation causes trouble in another.
