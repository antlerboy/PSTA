Children’s services in Islington

Who else was seeing the same children?

Chrissie Gregory and Sharon Davidson are improvement consultants who attended the National Commissioning Academy. In a November 2026 discussion, Chrissie described children in Islington whose needs came to the attention of several services. What had each service known about those children when help might have made a difference earlier?

Chrissie described bringing directors from the three services together to look at that question. Sharon took what practitioners had told her back to senior leaders and explained to staff what those leaders hoped to change.
