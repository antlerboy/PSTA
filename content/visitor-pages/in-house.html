In-house and local partnership programmes

The Academy runs courses for staff from one organisation or from several services working in the same place.

Designing the programme

Tell David Mason which decision or change brings the group together. For instance, a council and its providers might disagree about why a contract is missing its targets. Staff coming together after a reorganisation may need to agree who can make which decisions.

We’ll agree with you who should take part, what they can try between sessions, and what time their managers can give them. We’ll set out the proposed sessions, fees, and any further support in writing before you book.
