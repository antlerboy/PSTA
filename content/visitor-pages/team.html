A twelve-week programme for your team

One team of eight to twelve people works on a change in its own service, with time to test ideas between sessions.

Choosing the work

Agree the change with a senior sponsor who can make time for the team and get decisions made. A team might want to understand why people wait between two services and test an arrangement with the staff responsible for the handover.

During the twelve weeks

The team establishes what is happening now and what evidence it can collect. Colleagues rehearse a change, try it in the workplace, and return to compare results and decide what to do next. The sponsor reviews progress and any decisions the team needs help with.

The team keeps its test plan, decisions, and findings, including what didn’t improve. At the end it agrees what to do over the next 100 days. This record can inform a separate evaluation if the sponsor needs one.

Who does what

The Public Service Transformation Academy designs and facilitates the programme. The sponsor agrees access to staff, service information, and decisions. If the organisation needs consultants to help deliver a change, it can agree that work separately with RedQuadrant’s service change team or another partner.

Discuss a team programme

Email David Mason with a brief description of the service change and who would take part. We agree the design, timing, and fee with the sponsor before booking.
