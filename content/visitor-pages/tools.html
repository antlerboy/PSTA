Commissioning tools

Use these free question sets to examine how a public service is commissioned. Colleagues can answer the same questions separately and compare where their accounts differ.

Choose a Compass

smartCompass
Sixteen questions for someone who knows a place, service, or issue. Record an initial view you can discuss with colleagues.
Commissioning Compass
The full assessment covers eight aspects of commissioning in 79 questions. Record evidence for your scores, compare people’s answers, and agree what the team will review.
Compass v2.0 beta
A working edition with forty prompts, including who decides how resources are used and how residents experience those decisions.

In each Compass workspace you can keep a copy on your own device, download your answers, or print them for a meeting.

Adult social care

The adult social care commissioning question sheet was commissioned by the Local Government Association. It’s the predecessor of the full Commissioning Compass.
