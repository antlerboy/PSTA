Programmes

Join a national cohort as an individual or with colleagues, or commission a programme for your own organisation and its partners.

Commissioning

National Commissioning Academy
Work on a commissioning decision with people from other public services. The next cohort starts in January 2027. Places cost £2,490 excluding VAT, with a discount for groups of three or more.
Commissioning: a ten-step introduction
Three days for people new to commissioning. Sessions are usually two weeks apart so you can try what you’ve learned at work.
Commissioning and innovation: playing on a bigger stage
A three-day team programme. Compare how local people experience a service with what the organisations involved intend, then plan and test a change.
Commissioning simulation
Over two days, teams allocate a budget to simulated services and see what their decisions mean for other services and the people using them.
Commissioning health check
A questionnaire, interviews, and a half-day session for a leadership team reviewing one area of commissioning.
Contract Management Development Programme
For people managing contracts and provider relationships. Ask Sarah Johnston about places or an in-house group.

Leadership and service change

Systems Leadership Academy
For partners who report to different organisations and need to make a decision together. Four development days and three peer-learning sessions over four months.
Leading Transformation
Twenty-four modules on changing public services. Participants apply the course to their own work and rehearse decisions in a simulation.
Transformation Academies
Design a course for the people leading change in your organisation. For groups from several local services, see place-based and regional academies.
A twelve-week team programme
A team of eight to twelve works on one change and reviews the evidence with its sponsor.
Local Government Reorganisation Academy and Devolution Academy
Tailored programmes for people taking on new powers, reorganising councils, or deciding how services will be delivered during the transition.

Accredited training with Basis

Service Transformation Programme
Managers and practitioners use exercises and simulations to practise changing how a service works.
Agile Master in Public Services
Participants run their course work as an agile project and complete a short assessment. Basis delivers both programmes and the Academy accredits them.

Contact the Academy about a place, or arrange an in-house programme.
