Bring a live case
PSTA case clinic preparation

Use one page. Bring a question you can act on. Remove identifying details unless you have permission to share them.

1. What is happening, for whom?
Describe a concrete situation. Include the experience of people living with the consequences, and distinguish what you have observed from what you infer.

2. What is the question you need help with?
Name a choice, impasse, recurring pattern, or uncertainty. Say what you have already tried and what happened.

3. Who sees it differently?
Name the roles and interests that matter, including people affected but absent. What can each see that you cannot?

4. What can you change?
Describe your authority, relationships, resources, constraints, and access to evidence. Say where someone else's decision is needed.

5. What would count as a useful next move?
Suggest a small intervention, conversation, or test. What would support or challenge your expectation?

6. What can be shared?
State what is safe for the clinic, what must remain confidential, and who must agree before anything is published. Participation never implies permission to publish.

After the clinic
Record the move you chose, who will act, the review date, and the evidence you will look for. Return with what happened, including what did not work or changed your interpretation.
