A record of a change in practice

Case title:
Case-holder and organisation (only if cleared for publication):
Date and version:
Status: proposed / in progress / reviewed / cleared for publication

What was happening?
For whom, in what context, over what period? What was the starting evidence?

What did we think was producing it?
Distinguish observation, interpretation, assumption, and information we did not have. Include different accounts and people whose knowledge was missing.

What did we do?
Who took part? Who could decide? What changed in the work, relationships, rules, resources, or information? What did we decide against?

What happened?
Compare expected and observed effects. Separate proposed, identified, agreed, implemented, and sustained benefits. Record costs, effort, delay, error, exclusion, and effects elsewhere where known.

What supports that account?
List measures, records, observations, and participant accounts. State dates, scope, limitations, and alternative explanations. Do not imply that an outcome was caused by the intervention if the evidence cannot establish that.

What changed our understanding?
What did not work, surprised us, or made us redraw the boundary? What would we do differently?

What happened later?
Record a review date, who followed up, what continued, and what was lost or changed. Leave this blank until the follow-up has happened.

Who can check or challenge this account?
Record a suitable contact or correction route, with permission.

Publication permission
Who checked this record, what can be named, which artefacts can be shown, and when was permission given? Private participation and programme attendance do not confer permission to publish.
