A three-month case clinic
Operating template for a small public-service practice group

Agree the people, host, dates, format, confidentiality, access needs, and any fee before the group starts. This is a design to use or adapt, not a published event timetable.

Group and responsibility
Choose six live cases, with a person able to act on each. Use a named convenor and a host for each session. Six to eight people is a workable starting group. Invite differences of position and experience. Do not promise that the group represents everyone affected.

Working agreement
No sales pitches. No identifiable case material without permission. Ask the case-holder what help they want. Separate evidence, interpretation, and advice. Leave room to challenge the method, the facilitator, and the boundary. Participants decide what to act on. Publication requires a separate check and explicit agreement.

Suggested rhythm
Six 90-minute sessions, about a fortnight apart, over three months. Revisit live cases between sessions rather than filling the calendar with teaching. Alter the rhythm when the work requires it.

Month one: establish the inquiry
Sessions one and two introduce the six cases and work on the first two in depth. Define the system of interest, missing perspectives, concrete uncertainty, and one move each case-holder can make. Agree review dates and what evidence would alter the group's interpretation.

Month two: act and compare
Sessions three and four work on two more cases and revisit the first moves. Compare expectations with events. Look for displaced cost, risk, effort, or exclusion. Change the account when the evidence warrants it.

Month three: revisit and carry the work forward
Sessions five and six work on the remaining cases, then revisit all six. Decide what continues, changes, or stops. Name who will maintain the learning record. Agree whether any learning is ready to share beyond the group.

A 90-minute session
10 minutes: check in, access needs, and case-holder's question.
15 minutes: case account and questions for understanding.
20 minutes: other perspectives, evidence, assumptions, and missing voices.
20 minutes: possible moves and their conditions, consequences, and limits.
15 minutes: case-holder chooses a move, owner, evidence, and review date.
10 minutes: review how the group worked, and check commitments.

Public output
Choose a useful, specific practice note, diagram, tool correction, or anonymised case. State what happened, what remains uncertain, and the evidence available. Check permissions with the case-holder and the people or organisations concerned before publication. Nothing is published by default.

Use the Compass workspace to keep perspectives, decisions, and reviews together, or use another record the group can maintain.
